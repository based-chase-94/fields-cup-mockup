# Fields — cup mockup

Rotatable 3D mockups of the Fields beverage cups, for design review.

**Live:** https://based-chase-94.github.io/fields-cup-mockup/

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Tabs switch between designs; the fill toggle
previews the cup empty, with a light drink and with a dark one.

## Current SKU

**16 oz cold** — clear PET, 95 mm rim · 60 mm base · 132 mm tall.

## Notes

Cup dimensions are assumed industry standards, not manufacturer-confirmed. Every
design is re-projected onto whatever cone it is given, so real specs from a
supplier are a re-flow rather than a redo.

Artwork is not drawn flat onto the cup. A cup sidewall unrolls to an annular
sector rather than a rectangle, so each design is placed onto the cone piece by
piece — the per-design notes in the viewer record how each one was laid down and
why.

This repository holds the review site only. Dielines, print-ready artwork and the
projection toolchain are kept separately.
